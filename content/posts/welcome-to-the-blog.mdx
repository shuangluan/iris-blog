> _For years I've been posting slices of myself on other people's platforms. Red Notes takes the visual. LinkedIn takes the résumé version. WeChat is a graveyard I can't search. Each one holds a slice; `none of them holds the whole.` —
> This is the first place online that's actually mine. Here I can put the PM notes and the travel diaries and the middle-of-the-night thoughts and the side project write-ups in one place without first slicing myself into the shape whichever algorithm expects._

Hi, I'm **Iris**. I write about things that don't fit into a single tidy category, so this site doesn't try to pretend they do. There are five buckets:

- **Notes** — POV, reflections, small essays. The kind of thing you'd read on WeChat.
- **Case studies** — walk-throughs of things I've shipped or torn apart.
- **Travel notes** — half itinerary, half feelings.
- **US ⇄ CN life** — the weekly whiplash of living between two places.
- **Side projects** — small products that started as one bad idea on a Sunday.

## Two things I'd love

If you find something here you like:

<Callout tone="lime">
- Subscribe via RSS — one link, no algorithm, shows up when I actually publish. —
- Poke around the side projects — if you're the kind of person who builds things, we should talk..
</Callout>

That's it. No newsletter opt-in gate, no `welcome pack` email sequence. Just links and writing.

Enjoy. 🌍
